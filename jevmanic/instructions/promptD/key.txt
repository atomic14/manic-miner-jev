Willy is a miner in a platform game. The map shows the cavern, and
`map_legend` tells what each symbol means. `keys` gives facts about each key
or switch relative to Willy. Willy must collect all keys and then go into the
exit portal. Willy can climb only 2 rows with one jump. He can fall to a lower
floor, but after a long fall he cannot climb back. A crumbling floor breaks
when Willy uses it, so a way across a crumbling floor can be open only
once. Select the key or switch that Willy gets next, in an order that
lets him get all keys.
